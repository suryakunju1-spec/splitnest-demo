Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Terms of Service

SplitNest provides a shared roommate ledger to track household rent and utility balances. Creating an account gives you access to view your flat's ledger immediately without linking a bank account.

If you later choose to settle a bill by ACH transfer on the dashboard, you will provide separate, explicit ACH debit authorization at that time.

Privacy Policy

During registration, SplitNest collects only your name, email address, and your timestamped consent to these terms. We do not collect employer, date of birth, or upfront banking credentials at signup.

In this client-side demo, browser storage keeps only your consented identity, consent timestamps, and—if you link a bank later—masked last-4 account digits, never cleartext passwords or full bank numbers.
